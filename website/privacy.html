Privacy notice

A plain-language summary of what this website collects and how it is used.

What we collect

When you submit the contact form, a careers application or the client-portal login, we receive the information you type — typically your name, email address, phone number, company and message, and for applications your CV. The site also uses a web-analytics tool to measure visits and enquiry sources in aggregate.

How we use it

Form submissions are delivered to the official SS ERP Solutions mailbox and used solely to respond to your enquiry or application. We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for their own purposes.

Retention and contact

We keep correspondence for as long as needed to handle your enquiry and reasonable business records thereafter. To ask what we hold about you or to request deletion, write to support@sserpsolutions.net.

Cookies and chat

The site stores your dark/light theme preference in your browser. The embedded live-chat widget and WhatsApp click-to-chat are provided by third parties and apply their own privacy terms when you choose to use them.

This notice will be finalised with SS ERP Solutions before go-live.
