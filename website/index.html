Who We Are

Built on trust. Grown by referral.

SS ERP Solutions was founded in 2018 on a simple conviction: enterprise technology work should be done properly, or not at all.

We have grown entirely through trust: every engagement we hold today traces back to a client who trusted us with something difficult — and then told someone else. It is a patient way to build a company, and the right one. SAP is where our practice was born and where it runs deepest — the majority of our consultants, projects and training hours live in the SAP ecosystem.

Most of our clients are growing mid-size businesses that already run SAP and want more from it — reliability, speed and room to grow — without an enterprise-size IT department. We built our delivery model for exactly that — senior attention, honest scope and systems your team can actually run.
