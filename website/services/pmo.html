PMO

Programme and project management that keeps large initiatives honest: plans, risks, governance and decisions on the record.

Large programmes rarely fail from a single bad decision — they fail from a hundred small ones nobody wrote down. A disciplined PMO makes the state of the programme visible: what was decided, what it costs, what is at risk, and who owns the next move.

We run PMO as a service — from full programme management of an implementation to an independent PMO layer over your own delivery teams or another vendor's.

Our approach

  • A single plan that is real. One integrated plan with dependencies and capacity — not five decks that disagree.
  • Risks with owners and dates. A living risk register reviewed weekly, with escalation that actually escalates.
  • Decisions on the record. Every material decision logged with its context and alternatives — so the programme never re-litigates.
  • Reporting people read. One page per week for the sponsor: status, spend, risks, asks. No theatre.

Typical engagements

  • Full programme management of ERP implementations and rollouts
  • Independent PMO oversight of a system-integrator-led programme
  • Recovery management of programmes in trouble
  • Portfolio governance across parallel initiatives

What you can expect

  • A sponsor who always knows the true state of the programme
  • Fewer surprises, earlier — risks surfaced while they are cheap
  • An audit trail of decisions from kickoff to closure
