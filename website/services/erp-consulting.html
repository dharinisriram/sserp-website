ERP Consulting

Independent advice on the ERP you already run — health checks, optimisation roadmaps and honest answers — and clear-eyed selection when something new is genuinely needed.

An ERP decision outlives the people who make it. The platform you choose — and the processes you design around it — will shape how your organisation works for a decade. We help you make that decision with clear eyes: what you need, what it will really cost, and what it will take to land. Most of our clients are growing mid-size businesses making this call for the first time — so we explain everything, and assume nothing.

We are practitioners first. The consultants who write your roadmap are the same people who have implemented, migrated and supported these systems — so the advice is grounded in delivery reality.

Our approach

  • Process audit first. We map how your business actually operates today — order-to-cash, procure-to-pay, record-to-report — before any technology talk.
  • Requirements before vendors. Selection criteria come from your processes and growth plans.
  • Honest fit assessment. We tell you which gaps are configuration, which are customisation, and which should change your process instead.
  • A roadmap you can execute. Phasing, team shape, budget ranges and risks — written so your board can act on it.

Typical engagements

  • ERP strategy and platform selection for a first ERP or a replacement
  • S/4HANA readiness assessments and migration roadmaps
  • Health checks of existing implementations that underperform
  • Business-case development for transformation programmes

What you can expect

  • A decision your leadership can defend, with the trade-offs on paper
  • Realistic budget and timeline ranges before you commit
  • Reduced implementation risk because the groundwork happened first
