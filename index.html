Who We Are

Built on trust. Grown by referral.

SS ERP Solutions was founded in 2018 on a simple conviction: enterprise technology work should be done properly, or not at all.

We have grown entirely through trust: every engagement we hold today traces back to a client who trusted us with something difficult — and then told someone else. It is a patient way to build a company, and the right one.

From our roots in India we support organisations across Japan, the UAE and Singapore — and we are expanding globally, with a delivery model built to serve clients anywhere: implementing and optimising enterprise systems, running transformation programmes, and keeping mission-critical applications healthy long after go-live. We hold ourselves to high professional standards, and we commit only to work we are confident we can deliver.
